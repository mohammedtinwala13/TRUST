Known Security Boundaries & Limitations

  • OS-Level Keyloggers & Rootkits: Standard browsers cannot inspect kernel memory or OS-level keyboard drivers. Use the bank's on-screen virtual keyboard to mitigate.
  • Hardware Variations: Older single-core CPUs in rural cyber cafés may experience minor latency variance.
  • Browser Sandbox Permissions: Clipboard reading requires user permission or gesture on some browsers.
  • Risk Estimate Layer: TRUST is an explainable pre-flight risk detector, not a replacement for multi-factor authentication (MFA).
